# Product Charter — rumble-libre-ia

Status: Baseline delivered; deeper editorial workflow remains incremental.

## Mission

`rumble-libre-ia` is the Libre IA education and sharing surface for publishing essays, courses, resources, project explanations, and ecosystem learnings through a static-first Dioxus application.

## Initial Scope To Specify

- Content types: article, course, resource, project page, learning path.
- Editorial workflow.
- Publication workflow.
- Relationship to specs and artifacts produced by other Rumbles.
- Authoring and review roles.
- Static vs dynamic content model.
- SEO, accessibility, and archival requirements.

## Main Boundary

`rumble-libre-ia` must not become a generic CMS or internal workflow backend.
