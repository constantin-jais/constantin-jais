# Product Charter — rumble-cos

Status: Not started.

## Mission

`rumble-cos` is the personal education and sharing surface for publishing essays, courses, resources, project explanations, and ecosystem learnings.

## Initial Scope To Specify

- Content types: article, course, resource, project page, learning path.
- Editorial workflow.
- Publication workflow.
- Relationship to specs and artifacts produced by other Rumbles.
- Authoring and review roles.
- Static vs dynamic content model.
- SEO, accessibility, and archival requirements.

## Main Boundary

`rumble-cos` must not become a generic CMS or internal workflow backend.
